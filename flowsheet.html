Drawing notes

  1. Mass balance from WTT 26029 rev 1 (3-tunnel sheet). All flows within ±1 t.
  2. Turning always goes into a different, emptied tunnel: ⑩ empty T4, then ⑦ turn T1→T4, then ⑤ refill T1 the next working day.
  3. Detection bays hold shredded MSW at least 12 h under IR cameras (19–72 h in practice). Shred 06:00–11:00, fill from 07:00 next day. 4 fire-separated bays, pile 3.8 m, 0.5 m below the 4.3 m wall.
  4. Loader moves (L) need 1,986 h/yr against 1,600 h available. That means about 2,000 h on one machine, or 2 loaders. Shredder feeding is not included.
  5. SH-01 has 1,600 h/yr. Bulky waste at 60,000 t needs 3,000 h, so a second shredder is shown dashed as proposed.
  6. Batteries 1 kg/t is a planning figure. It needs replacing with an audit of the incoming MSW.
  7. Fire water: 6.6 l/min per m³ for 3 h (EA FPP style). Confirm the basis with NIEA and NIFRS.
