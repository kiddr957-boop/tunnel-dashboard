Drawing notes

  1. Mass balance from WTT 26029 rev 1 (3-tunnel sheet). All flows within ±1 t.
  2. Turning always goes into a different, emptied tunnel: L2 empties T4 ⑩, then L1 turns T1→T4 ⑦. T1 is refilled at 07:00 the next working day ⑤.
  3. SH-01 takes MSW only (46.9 t/h, 12:00–17:08). SH-02 takes bulky only and must reach ≥ 38 t/h to fit 1,600 h. Each machine backs up the other.
  4. No battery picking. BQ-01 Blazequel fire detection on the SH-01 outfeed belt (as at Portadown) catches ignitions at the shredder. The detection bays then catch delayed ignitions. All batteries (~75 t/yr at 1 kg/t) pass to the bays, tunnels and output.
  5. Detection bays: 4 × 343 m³ in 2 pairs, one pair per shredding day. Shredding in the afternoon and filling in the morning keeps the peak to one batch (481 t) and gives ≥ 13.9 h of watch.
  6. Output route: L2 empties stage 2 fast (~94 t/h) into OS-01 storage bays (3 × 320 m³, peak 336 t). L1 feeds the DH-01 dosing hopper (60 m³, ~38 min), which meters SP-01. In 1,600 h the line must average 32.8 t/h to clear WTT's 52,485 t/yr. At its rated 25 t/h it clears only 40,000 t/yr, so either it runs ~2,100 h or it needs a faster line. Build 2 needs about 66 t/h. The tunnel can't feed the hopper directly because the turn needs it empty in ~3.6 h.
  7. Loaders: with hopper feed, the total is 3,343 h/yr, so 2 loaders at ~1,670 h each (4% over 1,600) or 3.
  8. Heat plant sized to the staggered peak, 171 kW plus margin, not WTT's 257 kW. Fire water 1,632 m³ (stage 1 tunnel governs), with containment to match.
  9. Batteries 1 kg/t and the fire-water basis are interim. Replace them with Portadown detection and fire records and with NIEA/NIFRS guidance.
