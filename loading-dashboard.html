Model basis
  • Water evaporation is a % of the water entering each phase. Organics degradation is a % of the organic dry solids entering. Both default to the rates implied by the WTT sheet, so the balance scales with throughput.
  • Additional heat evaporates water at the latent heat set (default 2,500 kJ/kg, which reproduces WTT's −1,847 t/yr from 1,282,814 kWh/yr).
  • Batch size = annual tonnage ÷ (operating weeks × tunnels per week). Tunnels per week = active tunnels × 7 ÷ cycle days (the WTT method).
  • Dead length (1.5 m) is back-calculated from WTT's filling heights. It is the tunnel length not available for the pile (door and air-plenum zone).
  • Fire strategy: cells/t is a placeholder. Replace it with a battery audit of the incoming waste (the sample composition has 6–7% "Other / WEEE"). Composting doesn't destroy cells, so everything that isn't picked out leaves in the phase 2 output. The pile limit and fire-water rate (6.6 l/min per m³ for 3 h) follow the style of the English EA Fire Prevention Plan guidance. Coleraine is regulated by NIEA, so confirm which values apply.
  • Inputs marked assumed are not in the WTT sheet. Replace them with site data.
